Which Lightroom version do I need?

The presets work with Lightroom Classic (any version) and Lightroom Mobile (free app). Magic AI Tools require Lightroom Classic v12+ or the latest mobile version.

Do they work with my camera brand?

Yes — built and tested on Canon, but compatible with Sony, Nikon, Fujifilm, and all major cameras. You may need minor white balance or exposure tweaks depending on your system.

RAW or JPEG?

Both work. RAW files give the richest, most accurate results since the preset can access the full tonal range. JPEG works too, but with slightly less flexibility.

What are the Magic AI Tools?

Lightroom AI-powered local adjustments that target specific elements — subject, background, or sky — without affecting the rest of the image. Available in the Full Collection only.

How do I install the presets?

After purchase, you'll receive a link to the full installation guide with step-by-step instructions for both Lightroom Classic and Mobile.

What is Morning Haze?

An exclusive bonus preset included only in the Full Collection. It has built-in AI masks that automatically soften skin and brighten eyes — a preset and a retouch in one click.
